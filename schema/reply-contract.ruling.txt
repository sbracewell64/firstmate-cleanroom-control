Rules the consumer enforces mechanically (a violation is refused, never repaired):
  - additionalProperties is false on every object: an unknown or misspelled field
    name is REFUSED_MALFORMED and the refusal names the field.
  - schema: Compared by EXACT STRING EQUALITY, never by substring or prefix. 'fm-sol-control/v2'
      contains 'fm-sol-control', so a consumer matching on word overlap would consume
      retired-venue v1 traffic. See venue_isolation.match_rule in the canonical control config.
  - ruling_id: EXPLICIT AND UNIQUE, assigned by the ruler and unique across the ruler's whole
      lineage. Not derived by us: it is the ruler's own handle for its ruling, and
      supersedes/in_reply_to chains are built from it. Two rulings sharing one ruling_id are a
      lineage fork.
  - in_reply_to: MUST equal the request's request_id, by exact string equality. This is the
      explicit parentage: a ruling that does not name its parent request is not a ruling about
      it, however plausible its prose.
  - correlation_id: Derived; inputs and order owned by $defs.id_derivation. Venue unit is one
      issue per correlation_id, which bounds the comment universe so the completeness predicate
      is cheap and always satisfiable.
  - applies_to: EXACT RULING APPLICABILITY. Every field is copied from the request and compared
      by identity, never by ancestry, containment or plausibility. A disagreement on the subject
      is RULING_SUBJECT_MISMATCH; a disagreement on the policy digest is RULING_SUPERSEDED; a
      disagreement on the control config generation is REFUSED_STALE_CONFIG. The three never
      share a code, because they have three different repairs.
  - inspection: The ruler records WHICH evidence locators it actually resolved and read, and its
      own observations three-valued INCLUDING anything it could not observe. Under-claiming here
      is the honest shape; a ruling claiming every ref is the shape a fabricated ruling has.
  - directive: Closed and total over the reachable answers. THERE IS NO FIELD FOR A THIRD
      OPTION: the only way to reject every offered option is REJECT_ALL_WITH_CONSTRAINT, which
      produces no change and hands the constraint back. INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE is the ruler's
      could-not-observe and OUT_OF_SCOPE_CAPTAIN_RESERVED is the ruler correcting our boundary
      predicate; both are SUCCESSFUL round trips.
  - single_writer_assertion: The ruler asserting it is the only writer for this correlation id.
      A second ruling then contradicts an explicit claim rather than merely existing. An
      intentional replacement names the prior ruling_id in supersedes; two rulings that do not
      name each other are a LINEAGE FORK, which is a CNO: neither is consumed, both are
      preserved, and explicit supersession is required.
